# Kinetic Commerce AI - Information Architecture & Strategy

## 1. Product Vision
A scalable, AI-first commerce operating system that automates the entire retail lifecycle—from intelligent sourcing and demand prediction to personalized consumer shopping and automated vendor management.

## 2. Core User Flows
- **The Intelligent Shopper:** AI Search -> Semantic Filtering -> AI Review Summaries -> One-Tap Checkout.
- **The Power Merchant:** Automated Catalog Management -> AI Pricing Assistant -> Predictive Inventory Alerts.
- **The Platform Admin:** System-wide Monitoring -> Fraud Detection -> Global Analytics.

## 3. Sitemap & Page Hierarchy
### Public Marketplace
- **Home:** Personalized, AI-generated collections, trending hub.
- **AI Search & Category:** Semantic results with comparison engine.
- **Product Details:** 360 viewer, AI summary, price history, frequently bought together.
- **Checkout/Cart:** Multi-address, smart coupon application.

### Merchant & Enterprise
- **Seller Workspace:** Revenue analytics, AI SEO, demand forecasting.
- **Admin Command Center:** User roles, global logistics, security center.

## 4. Technical Strategy
- **Performance:** Edge rendering, streaming UI, skeleton states.
- **Design:** Kinetic Precision (Design System 1), WCAG AA compliant.
